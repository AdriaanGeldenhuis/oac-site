SUMMARY OF THE TEACHING BACKGROUND, THEME, TEXTS, AND OBJECTIVES 2024/2025

 

TEACHING BACKGROUND (Vanderbijlpark)

The teachings emphasize walking in the light of Jesus Christ through the Holy Spirit, guided by prophetic revelation. Despite worldly challenges, Apostolics are called to proclaim the gospel to promote love, peace, and reconciliation. Spiritual restoration involves returning to the first love, coming out of darkness into light, and remaining faithful to Christ.

 

THEME

As God's instruments of righteousness, through our unwavering faith in Jesus Christ and in fellowship with the Holy Spirit, we regard the things of the flesh as loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior.

 

TEXTS

PHILIPPIANS 3:1-21 & II CORINTHIANS 4:1-18

 

OBJECTIVES

By aligning ourselves with the life and teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ, we will:

 

  1. acknowledge God in all our considerations and actions, and allow Him to direct our paths by dedicating ourselves to be faithful to His Son, and glorifying Him in all that we say and do through our fellowship with the Holy Spirit.

 

  1. continue in prayer and supplication through our unwavering faith in God, proclaim the gospel of the Kingdom of God, which leads to a peaceful life. 

 

  1. equip the saints to remain steadfast in their faith in Jesus Christ and partake in all of God's promises, even in times of trials and tribulation. 

 

  1. endeavor to bring all Apostolics back to the first love by cultivating brotherly love and embracing small victories in Apostolic homes. 

 

  1. strengthen the gifts of the prophet (prophecies, visions, and dreams) in every Apostolic home. 

 

  1. fulfill the ministry of reconciliation by living in perfect love, peace, fellowship, and mutual support within the Body of Jesus Christ. 

 

PHILIPPIANS 3:1-21 

Encouragement of joy in Christ, rejecting confidence in the flesh and striving for spiritual maturity and heavenly citizenship.

 

Key points from this chapter:

  • Rejoice in faith and the Lord (Vs 1): Find joy and satisfaction in trusting God and celebrating His presence and goodness in every aspect of life.

 

  • Beware of False Teachers (Vs 2-3): Be vigilant and discerning against those who distort or misrepresent the truth of the Gospel for their own gain or misleading purposes.

 

  • The vanity of earthly achievements (Vs 4-6): Paul mentions his own religious credentials, which include being a Pharisee and living blamelessly under the law, but he considers it insignificant compared to the knowledge of Christ.

 

  • The surpassing value of the knowledge of Christ (Vs 7-11): The incomparable value of a personal relationship with Jesus, which far exceeds all earthly achievements and gains, bringing true righteousness, joy, and eternal life.

 

  • Strive for spiritual goals (Vs 12-16): Actively pursue a deeper relationship with God and personal growth in faith, character, and holiness.

 

  • Heavenly citizenship (Vs 17-21):  Acknowledge that our true home and ultimate loyalty is in heaven, where we belong to God's eternal kingdom, and we must live in a manner worthy of our calling.

 

II CORINTHIANS 4:1-18

Emphasizing perseverance in ministry, the power of the gospel, and the eternal perspective amidst suffering.

Key points from this chapter:

  • Perseverance in ministry (Vs 1): To steadfastly continue to serve and fulfill God's calling despite challenges and hardships, relying on His power and grace.

 

  • Integrity in preaching (Vs 2):  To deliver the Gospel honestly and transparently, without deception or distortion, and with a sincere heart.

 

  • Light of the Gospel (Vs 3-4):  The revelation of God's glory and truth through Jesus Christ, illuminating the path to salvation for all who believe. 

 

  • The Light of Christ (Vs 5-6): We serve as humble messengers of Christ and share the divine light and knowledge of God's glory that shines in us.

 

  • Treasure in Earthen Vessels (Vs 7): We are fragile vessels and imperfect, carrying the precious and powerful message of the Gospel, emphasizing that the true power of God comes, not from ourselves.

 

  • Endurance through suffering (Vs 8-9): Maintain faith and perseverance in the face of trials and hardships, trusting that God will provide strength and purpose through the challenges.

 

  • Living through death (Vs10-15):  By experiencing suffering and dying to ourselves, we manifest the life and resurrection power of Jesus Christ. Our suffering is not in vain, but serves to benefit others.

 

  • Eternal perspective (Vs 16-18): Focus on the eternal significance of our actions and experiences, valuing spiritual and eternal realities above temporal, worldly concerns.

Teaching background

The teachings of the Conference of Apostles (The COA) emphasize walking in the light of Jesus Christ through the guidance of the Holy Spirit and the prophetic voice. In a world full of trials, Apostolics are reminded that although they live in the world, they are not of it (John 17:14-16). The focus is on spiritual restoration, which involves:

  • Returning to the first love (Revelation 2:4-5)

 

  • Moving from darkness to light (1 Peter 2:9)

 

  • Remaining faithful to Jesus Christ (Hebrews 10:23)

 

This restoration is nurtured through brotherly love (Hebrews 13:1) and small victories in Apostolic homes, promoting unity and healing in the community 

(Psalm 133:1 and Ephesians 4:32).

 

Spiritual fruitfulness

We are taught that fruitfulness in the Spirit is not just about bearing fruit, but producing spiritual results through fellowship with the Holy Spirit (John 15:5). 

Without this fruit, Apostolics risk losing faith and falling short of God's grace.

 

Discipleship and faith

True disciples are called to:

  • Forget the past and move forward (Philippians 3:13-14)

 

  • Renew their faith (Romans 12:2)

 

  • Give glory to God for victories (1 Corinthians 15:57)

 

  • Serve with joy (Psalm 100:2)

 

This journey is supported by eternal solutions that help Apostolics fight the good fight of faith (1 Timothy 6:12).

God's protection and unity

Serving God brings joy and peace. His protection is a refuge in times of trouble 

(Psalm 91:1-2). Apostolics are encouraged to live in accordance with God's will and to care for every member of the Body of Christ (1 Corinthians 12:25-26 and Galatians 6:2).

 

Judgment and grace

There is no condemnation for those in Christ (Romans 8:1). Apostolics are urged not to judge others (Matthew 7:1-2), as judgment belongs to Jesus Christ, the light of the world (John 5:22 and John 8:12).
